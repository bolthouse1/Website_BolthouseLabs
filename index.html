Introducing

Your medical scans, transformed into something you can actually understand.

MyBodyPrism takes your existing medical imaging — CT, PET, MRI — and transforms it into stunning, interactive 3D visualizations. Install the desktop app and work with your files locally, upload to our secure cloud and explore through a premium streaming viewer, or stream immersive VR directly to your headset from anywhere.
